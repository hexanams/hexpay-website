ABOUT HEXPAY

We're building the operating system for African businesses.

Not another point solution. A single, intelligent platform that grows from payroll into everything a small or mid-sized business needs to run, without the cost or chaos of stitching five tools together.

OUR VISION

Digitalize how African businesses are run.

Our vision is to digitalize the way African businesses are run, and make them competitive. From a first hire to a hundredth, one account and one connected system that grows with the business instead of getting replaced by it.

OUR MISSION

Fewer tools. Better decisions.

To help businesses manage people, sales, payroll, finance and operations from one intelligent platform, so founders spend less time reconciling tools and more time growing.

WHERE WE STARTED

Payroll was never the whole point.

HexPay began as a way to solve one very specific, very painful problem for African businesses: paying people correctly, on time, and in line with tax law, without a finance team of ten.

But the deeper we went, the clearer it became. Payroll was never isolated. It touches hiring, leave, cash flow, sales performance and compliance all at once. Businesses weren't looking for a better payroll tool. They were looking for a better way to run the business.

So we kept building. Payroll became the entry point to something bigger: a connected operating system for how African businesses actually work.

HOW WE BUILD

Our approach

Three principles guide every module we ship.

01

Simple start

Every business starts with payroll, the module that's most urgent, most regulated, and hardest to get wrong. No forced onboarding into modules you don't need yet.

02

Modular growth

Sales, finance and inventory switch on when you're ready for them, same account, same data, no migration, no re-implementation.

03

Workflows, not features

We don't chase feature-list parity with the last tool you tried. We connect the workflows between modules, because that's where the real value is.
